How HyperFurion VK compares.

HyperFurion VK is an MIT-licensed voice keyboard for Linux and Windows. It types what you say into whatever app has focus, a terminal included; in a terminal it recognizes, no spoken phrase presses Enter. It ships no speech model: you bring a cloud key (xAI by default) or your own speech server. Here it is next to Talon, Numen, nerd-dictation and Dragon, including the rows where they win.

02Also worth a look

The table covers four tools, not the whole field. If you want a speech model in the box, or you’re on a Mac or a phone, look at these too.

  • Handy: free and open source (MIT) for Windows, macOS and Linux. Runs a Whisper or Parakeet model on your computer, fully offline, and pastes the text when you stop recording. Its Wayland support is limited and needs a helper such as wtype or dotool.
  • Windows Voice Access: built into Windows 11 (22H2 and later) and works without an internet connection, with “correct that” and “spell that”. Windows voice typing (Win+H) is built in too, but uses Microsoft’s online speech service.
  • Wispr Flow: for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, with a writing style per app. Transcription always happens in Wispr’s cloud. Free up to 2,000 words a week on a computer and 1,000 on a phone, then paid.
  • Superwhisper: for macOS, Windows, iOS and Android, with offline or cloud models and custom modes that set how it writes. Free and paid plans.
  • VoiceInk: open source (GPL-3.0) for macOS 15 and later, with offline models and modes that switch by app or website. The prebuilt app is paid, with a free trial.

03Where each one shines

Choose HyperFurion VK

You’re on Linux (Wayland or X11) or Windows and want to type by voice in any app, a terminal included, and correct by voice (“correct monday to friday”, “scratch that”). It only acts when you ask: it listens only after you press a hotkey or Kai’s on-screen button (Kai’s wake word is off until you turn it on), never presses Enter in a terminal it recognizes, and in the chat apps and chat sites it recognizes a spoken line break is Shift+Enter, so the message doesn’t send. You’ll need a speech provider key or your own speech server.

Choose Talon

You want the deepest hands-free control: coding by voice, a scripting language, eye tracking and noise commands, with a shared community command set. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux under X11, and Talon 1.0 is planned as its last public Linux release.

Choose Numen

You want a small, fully offline voice-control layer for Linux that works on Wayland, X11 and the text console, driven by short spoken words for keys, with a free Vosk model. Dictation is a phrase at a time.

Choose nerd-dictation or Dragon

nerd-dictation for a single-file, offline Python script on Linux that types as you speak and that you shape in Python (Vosk’s output is all lower-case). Dragon if you’re on Windows and want a paid dictation suite with its model in the box, voice correction, custom commands and MouseGrid.

04The short version

If you’re on Linux or Windows, want to type by voice in every app, terminal included, and can bring a speech key or run your own speech server, HyperFurion VK is built for that. For a speech model in the box, try Handy, Numen or nerd-dictation; for deep hands-free control, Talon; on a Mac or a phone, the apps above.
